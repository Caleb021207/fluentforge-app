# 08 — Spanish for English Speakers: Authoritative Course Map (Map of Record)

**Direction:** Spanish for English speakers (L1 = English, fixed; designed for anglophones from
scratch — NOT the ESL course reversed).
**Status:** consolidated planning document (map of record for this direction) · **Date:** 2026-07-30
**Compiled by:** ES Curriculum Architect, merging the four band plans (appendix A).
**Regional policy (owner-fixed, uniform course-wide):** coherent broadly-useful Latin American
standard — seseo; `ustedes` as the only productive plural "you"; `vosotros` receptive-only,
labeled *(Spain)*; one productive form per lexical-variant set, variants flagged receptively
(≤1 flag/lesson, never tested productively).
**Labels:** **[proposed]** design decision open to revision · **[assumption]** dependency on
another team/data · **[requires validation]** needs SME/data/pilot check · **[estimated]**
planning estimate with stated assumptions, never a guarantee.

This file supersedes the four WIP band plans as the single authoritative course map. The band
plans remain the appendix references for lesson-level detail, sample lessons, and full AI
interaction/mission contracts (§13 of each). Where this file and a band plan disagree, this
file wins; every such disagreement is documented in §2.

**Owner-target evaluation [proposed]:** 1,800–2,200 is the right magnitude for strong A2
*provided* (a) multiword chunks count as items — they carry the functional load at this level

pressure to pad toward 2,200 with topical nouns that don't improve the graduation conversation.

override in both directions [requires validation: licensing + list vintage].

### 4.2 Selection principles (unified from bands)

1. Frequency-first with survival override (scenario-critical low-frequency admitted: `andén`,
   `talla`, `jarabe`; high-frequency without an A2 job deferred).
2. Chunks are first-class FSRS cards, taught unanalyzed-first.
3. LatAm productive default; variants flagged receptively, once.
4. Nouns stored and drilled with article; gender exceptions carry a note (`el agua (f.)`).
5. Cognate leverage with false-friend quarantine; every cognate card carries `cognate_status`
   and (from U11) a stress-position field.
6. Load ceilings ≤10/≤4 (R12).
7. Strategy language outranks topic language in U12.
8. Tense-partner forms live on one card family per lemma (preterite/imperfect variants keyed to
   `vocabulary.lemma`).

### 4.3 Recycling plan — the structural mechanism

Owner requirement: every important item must recur across **comprehension, retrieval,
listening, writing, controlled speaking, guided interaction, spontaneous conversation, and
spaced review**. This is enforced structurally, not aspirationally:

| # | Context | Structural mechanism (where reuse is *forced* by design) | Enforcement artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comprehension | Intro lesson: ≥2 receptive exercises per item (flashcard-R, listening_mc, matching) | `vocab_lesson_map` + lint V1 (§10) |
| 2 | Retrieval | Same lesson: ≥1 productive exercise (cloze/translation/productive flip); FSRS productive card created same-day | lint V1; `vocab_card_state` |
| 3 | Listening | The ≥95%-known constraint on all passages makes authored passages *consume* prior vocab; rule: each core item appears in ≥2 passages within 2 units of introduction | passage-composition linter V2 |
| 4 | Writing | Writing-task specs enumerate required lexical sets; W-AI whitelists are drawn from taught inventory | interaction contract `assumed_vocabulary`; lint V3 |
| 5 | Controlled speaking | ASR read-aloud/dialogue-completion lines are generated from each item's `example_sentence` | authoring contract field reuse |
| 6 | Guided interaction | Every S-AI/W-AI contract lists `assumed_vocabulary`; rule: each core item named in ≥1 interaction contract within its band | lint V3 cross-check |
| 7 | Spontaneous conversation | Missions are cumulative **by construction** (goal cards require multi-unit language); graduation vocab probe is stratified across all 12 units and card ages | mission contracts; G-E1 spec |
| 8 | Spaced review | FSRS from day of introduction; warm-up composition gradient (R8) guarantees old-unit sampling forever; error-fed review re-injects lapsed items | `vocab_card_state`; warm-up scheduler |

**Operational gate [proposed]:** a `vocab_exposure_matrix` (derived view over authored content,
§9) must show each core item planned into **≥6 of the 8 contexts** across the course — with
listening and spaced review mandatory — before the item's unit can move `gated → operational`.

**Worked example — `la cuenta` (introduced es-5.6):** comprehension+retrieval in es-5.6
(E2/E3 matching + E4 cloze + productive flashcard); FSRS card same-day, resampled by warm-ups

## 8. Assessment architecture (consolidated, testable)

Evidence model: all results write to `assessment_evidence` [proposed extension, §9] linked to
`can_do_statements` rows (CEFR + NCSSFL-ACTFL); learner-facing reports list can-dos with
evidence, not scores alone. No claim is ever derived from XP/streaks/completion/time.

### 8.1 Band Gate (after U3) — rehearsal-weight gate

Evidence (all four): (1) FSRS stability ≥4 days on ≥80% of the 220-item band core [requires
validation]; (2) ≥70% on a 24-item mixed cumulative set (≥1/3 productive); (3) 4–5-min guided
AI conversation — ≥6/8 questions answered comprehensibly, ≥2 questions asked back, ≥1 repair on
a planted fast turn; (4) es-3.16 writing at rubric ≥2/3 per axis. Outcomes: `pass` /
`pass_with_review` (one near-miss on 1–2) / `remediate` (targeted micros → retake failed
components only). Second failure: repeat es-3.17–3.18 with new variants; no hard lockout.

### 8.2 Checkpoint 1 (after U5) — certifies U1–U5

~35 min, ≤2 sittings. E1 vocab: 60-card stratified probe ≥80%. E2 grammar: 24-item adaptive set
≥70%. E3 listening: 2 single-play passages, ≥6/8 incl. 2 price captures. E4 spoken: 5–7-min
adaptive interview across 5 can-do tasks (intro / routine / person / directions / order a
meal) — ≥4/5 completed at scaffolding ≤2, intelligibility ≥ threshold, ≥2 learner-initiated
repairs or follow-ups. E5 writing: 40–60-word dinner-arrangement message at rubric
"functional"; accent-sensitive on top-10 tilde words (R10). Outcomes: `pass` /
`pass_with_review` (exactly one below bar, E4 ≥3/5; re-probe day 7) / `remediation` (blocks
U6; shortened CP1′ on failed components; 2nd loop → coach-review flag). CP1 must be attempted
before U6 unlocks.

### 8.3 Checkpoint 2 (after U8) — certifies U7–U8 before the preterite

~35 min. T1 interpretive ≥7/10 (announcement, pharmacy dosage grid, se-puede sign). T2
interpersonal spoken: adaptive chain (travel problem → pharmacy) with one engineered repair
moment and one register switch; rubric 4 criteria × 0–3, pass = ≥2 on every criterion. T3
written: 40–60-word cancel/reschedule message, rubric 3 × 0–3, ≥2 each. Entry gates: 7.18 +
8.19 complete; FSRS ≥75% on the U7–U8 tagged subset; skill_mastery ≥0.6 on GS-47/50/51/52.
Outcomes: `pass` / `pass_with_review` (one criterion at 1; re-check in R9-review) /
`remediation` (max 2 retakes with fresh forms, then alternate-evidence portfolio — no learner
is hard-walled).

### 8.4 Graduation (after U12) — certifies the whole-course definition of done

**Eligibility:** all U10–U12 lessons complete; all three band missions ≥ `achieved_with_support`;
es-12.17 completed; es-12.20 mock segment done (12.18 readiness screen advises timing).

**Components:**

| # | Component | Instrument | Bar [proposed] |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-E1 | Vocabulary | 100-card stratified probe across U1–U12 (unit × card-age × chunk mix; 60% productive) | ≥80% |
| G-E2 | Grammar in context | 30-item adaptive set (tense selection in context, ser/estar, gustar-type, hace…que, saber/conocer, embedded questions, agreement under load) | ≥70% |
| G-E3 | Listening | 3 single-play passages (~150/155/160 wpm) incl. one unknown-word-tolerance item; 12 items | ≥9/12 |
| G-E4 | **The Conversation** | ~15-min adaptive AI conversation, supportive interlocutor, single sitting | 4 gates below |
| G-E5 | Writing | 80–120-word connected message (apologize–explain past–propose future), in-app, no aids | rubric "functional"; ≥3 connectors; coherent time frames; register; accent-sensitive standard tilde list |

**G-E4 phases (all six must be covered by the adaptive engine):** ① self/family/work/interests
② past narration (≥3 connected events) ③ future plan + goal ④ embedded transaction (U4–U8
domain) ⑤ planted twist forcing repair ⑥ reciprocity (≥3 learner follow-up questions, woven).

**G-E4 gates:** *Task* — ≥5/6 phases at scaffolding ≤2 (level 3 = phase not passed). *Interaction*
— ≥3 learner-initiated follow-ups AND ≥2 successful repair sequences (≥1 self-initiated); a full
es-12.19 mission success pre-credits 1 repair [proposed]. *Intelligibility* — ASR+rubric
composite ≥ threshold [requires validation]; accent never penalized. *Stamina* — ≥13 min
sustained; ≤2 collapses to English beyond the permitted 12.15 strategy use.

**Outcome logic (owner's four outcomes):**
- All components at bar → **`graduated`** (can-dos marked achieved with evidence links;
  post-course conversation-tool profile initialized).
- Exactly one of G-E1/E2/E3/E5 below bar AND G-E4 fully passed → **`graduated_with_review`**
  (targeted queue + re-probe day 10).
- G-E4 fails task or intelligibility gate, or ≥2 other components fail → **`remediation`**
  (mapped micros + shortened retake of failed components; G-E4 re-run is a fresh scenario;
  second loop → coach-review flag).
- G-E4 passes task+intelligibility but fails the interaction gate → **`repeat_missions`**
  (2 fresh mission variants with repair/follow-up success criteria, then re-take G-E4 only).

### 10.4 Localization rules (this direction)

  appears in instructional strings only inside marked example spans.

  Spanish-graded-to-band, LatAm standard per regional policy.
