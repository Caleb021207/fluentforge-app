# Palavox Overhaul — Spanish-for-English-Speakers Curriculum Plan: Late A2 Band (Units 10–12 + Graduation)

**Direction:** Spanish for English speakers (L1 = English, fixed; designed for anglophones from scratch — NOT the ESL course reversed)
**Band:** Late A2 — Unit 10 (Plans & future), Unit 11 (Work & study), Unit 12 (Repair & survive), **Graduation**
**Status:** draft (WIP) · **Author:** ES late-A2 band planner subagent · **Date:** 2026-07-30
**Regional policy (owner-fixed):** coherent broadly-useful Latin American standard — seseo, `ustedes` as the productive plural; `vosotros` receptive-only, labeled *(Spain)*; major lexical variants flagged (`carro/coche`, `computadora/ordenador`, `manejar/conducir`) without drowning learners.
**Progressive-release ratios for this band (owner guidance):** 25–35% structured / 35–45% guided interaction / 25–35% open conversation

**Band headline numbers [proposed]:**
- **56 core lessons** (U10: 18, U11: 18, U12: 20) + **Graduation event** (not counted as a core lesson)
- **520 new lexical items** incl. **142 multiword chunks** (U10: 168, U11: 182, U12: 170) → course cumulative ≈ **1,840** [assumption-dependent, §4]
- **~35.5 hours** total active engagement (≈2,120 min median learner), split ≈ **30/41/29** across structured / guided / open — inside owner guidance
- **≥7 embedded AI interactions per unit** (≥4 spoken, ≥2 written) + 1 unscripted mission per unit + the ~15-min adaptive **Graduation conversation**

Labels used throughout: **[proposed]** = design decision open to revision; **[assumption]** = dependency on another band/team; **[requires validation]** = needs SME/data/native-speaker check before gating to `operational`. Lesson IDs follow the `es-10.1` convention of the ES late-A1 plan (the ES early-A1 plan uses `ES-A1E-U1-L01`; harmonization is a cross-band authoring-schema task **[requires validation]**).

## 0. Position in the course and interfaces with adjacent bands

- **[assumption]** The ES early-A2 band (U7 Transport, U8 Health, **Checkpoint 2**, U9 Past experiences) delivers before this band starts: **preterite** of regular verbs + core irregulars (`ir/ser, hacer, estar, tener, poder`), first narrative sequencing (`primero, luego, después, ayer, la semana pasada`), **`ir a + infinitivo`** introduced productively for near-future, **`estar + gerundio`**, indirect-object pronouns beyond the `gustar` pattern, reflexive daily-routine verbs consolidated, body/health/pharmacy lexicon, transport/travel lexicon, `hay que / tener que` obligation, and Checkpoint 2 (certifying U6–U8) passed or passed-with-review. Every prerequisite in §3 pointing into U7–U9 depends on this list.
- **[assumption]** Cumulative vocabulary entering this band ≈ **1,320** items (U1–U3 ≈ 370 + U4–U6 = 455 + U7–U9 ≈ 495). This band's 520 lands the course at ≈ 1,840 — inside the owner's 1,800–2,200 target. If early A2 delivers materially fewer, §4's ⊕ expansion pools absorb the gap.
- **This band owns:** consolidated productive `ir a + inf` planning talk; intention/hope verbs (`pensar/querer/esperar + inf`); real conditional `si + present`; `quedar` (arranging to meet); **imperfect** (forms + habitual/descriptive uses) and a *receptive-only* preterite/imperfect contrast; `saber vs conocer`; `hace + time + que` duration; impersonal `se` for language management; indirect questions; discourse markers and the full repair/circumlocution toolkit; morphological simple future **receptive-only** (labeled preview). Full preterite/imperfect *productive* contrast, subjunctive, and commands (productive) are **explicitly out of scope** — B1 material for the post-course conversation tools. **[proposed]**
- **Graduation** follows U12 and is the course's terminal gate (owner skeleton). It certifies the whole-course communicative definition of done, not this band alone (§12).
- **Lesson-count fit:** A1 ≈ 102–106 **[assumption]**; if early A2 lands at ~64–70 lessons, A2 totals 120–126 — inside the owner's ≈120–150 A2 range. If early A2 comes in under 64, this band's ⊕ expansion candidates (§2 footer) raise it to 60 without padding; if A2 overflows 150, ⊖ trim candidates cut this band to 52. **[proposed]**
- **Not-a-mirror check:** the band's spine is anglophone-specific — imperfect vs preterite has no English morphological analog (English *used to / would / was -ing* map is taught explicitly); `saber/conocer` splits English *know*; `hace…que` replaces English present-perfect duration (*I've worked here for 3 years* → present tense in Spanish); embedded-question word order (no *do*-support, no inversion); cognate stress transfer (`universidad`, `importante`) is an English-specific intelligibility risk. `vocabulary.translation_l1` single-column remains sufficient for this direction (L1 fixed = English). **[proposed]**
- **Exports to post-course tools:** U12's repair/circumlocution toolkit, discourse markers, and conversation-management chunks are the explicit foundation for the B1-direction conversation tools; Graduation's saved error categories seed the post-course remediation loop (§13e).

## 1. Refined unit definitions

All CEFR citations are condensed from the CEFR Companion Volume (2020) descriptor scales; exact scale placement **[requires validation]** against the published tables. ACTFL references: ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines + NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements. Exit target for the band: **strong A2/A2+ (CEFR), Intermediate Low with emerging Intermediate Mid features (ACTFL)**.

### Unit 10 — Planes y futuro / Plans & future ("Make it happen later")

- **Theme:** weekend and holiday plans, invitations, arranging and re-arranging meetings and appointments, weather as a planning constraint, personal goals.
- **Key language use:** interpersonal negotiation of joint plans; transactional scheduling; polite refusal.
- **Content objective:** negotiate a complete plan with another person (what/when/where), issue and respond to invitations with culturally appropriate softening, reschedule when something falls through, state medium-term goals.
- **Language objective:** `ir a + inf` consolidated across persons/negation; `pensar/querer/esperar + inf`; `si + presente` conditionals; `quedar` arrangements; future-time adverbials; weather constructions; simple future receptive; linking/sinalefa in connected speech.
- **CEFR anchors:**
  - *Sustained monologue: describing experience*, A2+: "can describe plans and arrangements, habits and routines, past activities and personal experiences" — the unit's presentational exit.
  - *Conversation*, A2: "can make and respond to invitations, suggestions and apologies"; *Planning-type informal discussion*, A2: "can make and respond to suggestions, agree and disagree" and "discuss what to do in the evening/at the weekend."
  - *Using telecommunications*, A2: "can use telecommunications with friends to exchange simple news, make plans and arrange to meet."
  - *Correspondence/written interaction*, A2: "can write short, simple notes and messages relating to matters of immediate need"; simple personal messages "expressing thanks and apology" (extended here to invitations/declines).
- **ACTFL modes:** Interpretive — Intermediate Low: understand main idea + key details of voicemails/forecasts on familiar topics. Interpersonal — Intermediate Low: "can create with the language" to negotiate plans in sentence-level discourse, ask and answer questions. Presentational — Novice High→Intermediate Low: connected spoken/written description of upcoming plans and goals.
- **Refinement vs owner skeleton:** unchanged in theme; explicitly carries the *rescheduling/plan-failure* strand (feeds U12 repair) and caps morphological future at receptive. **[proposed]**

### Unit 11 — Trabajo y estudio / Work & study ("Talk about what you do — and did")

- **Theme:** jobs and workplaces, studies and school talk, abilities and skills, work/study history, job ads and a simple interview, aspirations.
- **Key language use:** presentational self-description across time (present job → past history → future goals); the course's most sustained `usted`-register interaction (interview).
- **Content objective:** present yourself professionally: describe current work/studies and duties, state abilities, narrate work/study history, describe how things *used to be*, handle a routine screening interview, write a mini-bio.
- **Language objective:** `ser + profession`; `trabajar de/en/para`; `saber vs conocer`, `saber + inf`; `hace + time + que + present`; preterite consolidation in CV narrative; **imperfect** (regular + `era/iba/veía`) for habitual/descriptive past; receptive `era/fue` contrast; cognate stress.
- **CEFR anchors:**
  - *Describing experience*, A2: "can describe his/her family, living conditions, educational background, present or most recent job" — near-verbatim the unit's content objective.
  - *Interviewing and being interviewed*, A2: "can make him/herself understood in an interview and communicate ideas and information on familiar topics, provided he/she can ask occasionally for clarification."
  - *Information exchange*, A2: "can ask and answer questions about habits and routines… pastimes and past activities."
  - *Overall written production*, A2: "can write a series of simple phrases and sentences linked with simple connectors like 'and', 'but' and 'because'" — the mini-bio bar.
- **ACTFL modes:** Interpretive — Intermediate Low: job ads, short bios, interview questions. Interpersonal — Intermediate Low: interview Q&A with clarification requests. Presentational — Intermediate Low: paragraph-level spoken/written self-presentation across three time frames (with formulaic scaffolds; full time-frame control is not claimed — that is an Advanced ACTFL criterion).
- **Refinement vs owner skeleton:** owner's "Work & study" gains the band's tense capstone (imperfect) because childhood/education/work-history talk is the natural communicative home for habitual past; this keeps grammar tied to need rather than adding a "grammar unit." **[proposed]**

### Unit 12 — Reparar y sobrevivir / Repair & survive ("Stay in the conversation")

- **Theme:** communication breakdown and recovery, describing unknown words (circumlocution), phone calls, problems (lost items, mix-ups, complaints), emergencies-lite, keeping a conversation going.
- **Key language use:** interaction management and repair as *content*; integration of the whole course under mild pressure.
- **Content objective:** survive authentic friction: signal non-understanding precisely, request repetition/slowing/spelling, paraphrase around unknown words, repair after being misunderstood, manage a phone call, resolve a problem end-to-end, sustain a 10-minute supported conversation.
- **Language objective:** impersonal `se` (`¿cómo se dice…?`); circumlocution frames (`es una cosa que sirve para…`); indirect questions (`¿Sabe si…?`, `¿Me puede decir dónde…?` — embedded order, no inversion); discourse markers (`bueno, pues, entonces, o sea, es que`); reformulation frames (`quiero decir…, o sea…`); rhythm/fluency and Spanish fillers.
- **CEFR anchors:**
  - *Asking for clarification*, A2: "can ask very simply for repetition when he/she does not understand" and "can ask for clarification about key words or phrases not understood, using stock phrases."
  - *Compensating*, A2: "can use an inadequate word from his/her repertoire and use gesture to clarify"; B1 on-ramp: "can define the features of something concrete for which he/she can't remember the word" — U12 targets this *with frames provided*.
  - *Turntaking*, A2: "can use simple techniques to start, maintain or close a short conversation"; "can initiate, maintain and close simple face-to-face conversation."
  - *Monitoring and repair* (B1, receptive target only): "can ask for confirmation that a form used is correct" — modeled by the AI, elicited but not required.
  - *Overall spoken interaction*, A2+: "can interact with reasonable ease in structured situations and short conversations, provided the other person helps if necessary" — the graduation-adjacent bar.
- **ACTFL modes:** Interpretive — Intermediate Low/Mid: natural-feature listening (false starts, backchannels) for gist + key detail. Interpersonal — Intermediate Low: sustained task-based exchanges with self-initiated repair. Presentational — Intermediate Low: written problem reports and connected messages with discourse markers.
- **Refinement vs owner skeleton:** unchanged in theme; made the *terminal integration unit* — 20 lessons (band max) because it owns graduation rehearsal, the chained mission, and the course-wide spiral review. **[proposed]**

### ▶ Graduation (after U12 — certifies the whole course; design in §12)

~15-min adaptive AI conversation + component gates → `graduated | graduated_with_review | remediation | repeat_missions`. Proficiency claims derive **only** from performance evidence — never XP/streaks/completion/exposure/time (owner mandate).

**Owner-target evaluation [proposed]:** 1,800–2,200 is right for strong A2 *provided* chunks are counted as items (they carry the functional load at this level) and provided ~15% of the total is receptive-only (variants, previews, sign language). Recommendation: hold ≈1,850 core + an "encountered, not owned" receptive pool (~150–250) logged but never gating.

## 6. Pronunciation progression (targets, minimal pairs, intelligibility rationale)

Philosophy: **intelligibility, not accent elimination** (inherited). One [LAB] per unit + micro-drills inside every speaking exercise; ASR thresholds tuned per target **[requires validation with pilot audio]**. This band shifts from segmentals (owned by A1/early A2) to **connected speech, stress, and fluency** — the things that break a 15-minute conversation.

| Stage | Unit | Target | Minimal pairs / drill items | Intelligibility rationale |
|-------|------|--------|-----------------------------|--------------------------|
| P7 | 10.7 | Linking/sinalefa across word boundaries; vowel purity maintained at speed | `voy‿a‿ir, va‿a‿estar, mi‿hijo, ¿qué‿hora‿es?`; contrast choppy EN word-by-word delivery | Learners who pause between every word become unintelligible *listeners* too — linking training doubles as listening decoding for 10.11/12.13 |

## 12. Graduation design (after U12 — certifies the whole course)

**Purpose:** evidence that the learner meets the owner's communicative definition of done: sustain a simple ~15-min conversation on familiar topics with a supportive interlocutor; self/family/work/routines/interests; basic past experiences and future plans; transactions (food/shopping/travel/directions/scheduling/immediate needs); ask + answer follow-ups; request repetition/clarification; reformulate when misunderstood; understand paced speech; be intelligible with an accent; write simple connected messages. Claims map to CEFR A2/A2+ and NCSSFL-ACTFL Intermediate Low can-dos with evidence links; **never** XP/streaks/completion/exposure/time.

**Eligibility:** all U10–U12 lessons complete, all three band missions at ≥ `achieved_with_support`, 12.17 completed, 12.20 mock segment done. 12.18 readiness screen advises timing. **[proposed]**

**Format:** ~55 min total across ≤2 sittings **[proposed]**; the conversation (G-E4) must be a single unbroken sitting.

| # | Component | Instrument | Pass bar [proposed] |
|---|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| G-E1 | Vocab retention | 100-card stratified probe across U1–U12 (by unit, card age, chunk/word mix; 60% productive) | ≥80% |
| G-E2 | Grammar in context | 30-item adaptive set: tense selection in context, ser/estar, gustar-type, hace…que, saber/conocer, embedded questions, agreement under load | ≥70% |

**E5 · ordering** — difficulty 3 · P · ~1 min · vocab v5, v6, v2.

Key = shown order. Rationale: the three-part decline shape (regret → reason → alternative) is the lesson's pragmatic core.

**E6 · dictation** — difficulty 3 · R→P · ~1.5 min · vocab v1.

**E7 · translation (EN→ES)** — difficulty 4 · P · ~1.5 min · vocab v3, v2.

**E8 · dialogue (controlled completion, branching)** — difficulty 3 · P · ~2.5 min · vocab v1–v8.
